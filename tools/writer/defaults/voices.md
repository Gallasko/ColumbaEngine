# Voices

Every chapter is drafted once in each voice below. Edit, add or remove them; each is a "## name"
section saying how it writes.

## guide

Step by step, speaking to the reader as "you". Plain and direct: short sentences, one idea at a
time, each step shown before it is named.

## story

First person: how it was found, what was tried first and failed, what the numbers said. The
explanation comes through the story, never instead of it.

## brief

Numbers and code first, then the few sentences that explain them. Short: nothing the reader could
skip.
