# How these articles teach

## The reader

The default reader knows a programming language well and does not know ColumbaEngine. An
article's plan may name another reader; then that reader holds for it.

## Core and general

- The article's **core** is explained in full, every step: what it is, why it matters, how it works
  here, what changed. Anything that exists only in ColumbaEngine or PgScript is core.
- **General programming** (loops, functions, hash maps, pointers, recursion) is never explained,
  unless it is the article's very subject: a loop optimization article explains how the VM runs a
  loop.

## The thread

- One question is carried through the article.
- Each chapter is one step of the answer, in an order where nothing is used before it is explained.
- A chapter: why it matters, a concrete example, the explanation, what to keep.
- Each chapter opens by saying how it answers what the previous one left open.
